About the Academy

A Strong Foundation for Every Young Player

At Knight's Vision Chess Academy, we offer fun and educational chess classes for kids of all levels. Our programs focus on building strategic thinking and problem-solving skills in a supportive environment – with personalized coaching every step of the way.

Structured Curriculum

Well-planned, systematic training tailored to each student's level.

Personalized Coaching

Individual attention so every child grows at their own pace.

Strategic Thinking

Lessons that build planning, focus and problem-solving for life.

Supportive Environment

Friendly, professional coaching that keeps chess fun for every child.
